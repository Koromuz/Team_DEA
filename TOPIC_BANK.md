# Research Topic Proposals
 
## TOPIC I. Understanding Students' Online Attentiveness Using Computer Vision

 
**Idea:**
Develop a computer vision system that analyzes student attentiveness and engagement during online classes.

 
**Problem:**
As online learning continues to be adopted by educational institutions, maintaining student attentiveness has become a significant challenge. Students are exposed to numerous distractions while studying from home. This study aims to address this issue by developing a computer vision-based software solution that can detect student attentiveness and engagement during online classes.

 
**Closest Existing Work:**
*Real-Time Student Attention Monitoring in Classrooms Using Computer Vision* by Abdul Moeid and Mokammel Haque

 
**Data Needed:**
- Image datasets of students attending online classes
- Video datasets of students participating in online learning sessions

 
**Compute Needed:**
- Cloud GPU environment with NVIDIA GPUs for model training and fine-tuning
- Webcam for real-time testing and evaluation

 
**Biggest Risk:**
- Data privacy and ethical concerns regarding continuous webcam monitoring
- False positives caused by environmental factors, lighting conditions, and camera quality

## TOPIC II. Development of an Alternative System for the Current NU Professor Queuing System

 
**Idea:**
Design and develop an improved version of the current NU Professor Queuing System that offers a better user experience and more efficient queue management.

 
**Problem:**
The current NU Professor Queuing System presents usability challenges for students. The researchers aim to develop a more robust, user-friendly, and intuitive queuing system that addresses the shortcomings of the existing solution.

 
**Closest Existing Work:**
The current NU Professor Queuing System located in front of the faculty offices.

 
**Data Needed:**
- Student surveys
- Faculty surveys
- Usability feedback regarding pain points of the current system

 
**Compute Needed:**
- Standard cloud hosting platform
- Real-time database infrastructure

 
**Biggest Risk:**
- Low adoption rate among users
- Resistance from faculty members and administrators to replace the existing system

## TOPIC III. AI chatbots Performance Degradation long term

 
**Idea:**
Creating solution for model performance degradation.

 
**Problem:**
AI chatbots performance gradually gets worse as long term the models answers get less accurate, relevant, or reliable over time.

 
**Closest Existing Work:**
*CHEEM framework* (Continual Hierarchical‑Exploration‑Exploitation Memory) from North Carolina State University

 
**Data Needed:**
- Comprehensive and Representative Dataset: 
- Diversity and Augmentation
- Fresh and Updated Data

 
**Compute Needed:**
- CUDA‑capable GPUs with high tensor cores and large VRAM.
- VRAM that can accommodate the model parameters plus active batch sizes.
- High‑bandwidth memory (HBM)
- High‑speed networking ( InfiniBand, 400 GbE) for distributed training

 
**Biggest Risk:**
- Project cost and materials
- Available data for improving retention of a model

## TOPIC IV AI-Powered Career Guidance System In The Philippines.

 
**Idea:**
A system that uses AI to assess a student's capabilities, predict their potential, and identify their specific strengths in relation to a particular course or academic program.

 
**Problem:**
Many students struggle with choosing an academic course that aligns with their interests, abilities, and strengths. This leads some college students to repeat academic years or shift programs due to unsuitable course choices, while graduating Senior High School students face uncertainty in selecting a college course that matches their capabilities and future career goals.

 
**Closest Existing Work:**
AI-powered Career Guidance System
Survey paper-AI powered career recommendation For Students

 
**Data Needed:**
* Student Profile Data
* Academic Performance Data
* Skills & Abilities
* Students' Interest & Preferences
* Student Academic History
* Expert or Guidance Counselor Assessment

 
**Compute Needed:**
* Cloud-based hosting platform for system accessibility.
* AI and machine learning infrastructure for analyzing student data and predicting suitable college courses.
* Real-time database infrastructure for storing and retrieving student profiles, grades, skills, and assessment results.
* Data processing capabilities for evaluating student performance and identifying strengths and weaknesses.
* Secure data storage and backup systems to protect student academic records.
* Stable internet connection for students, administrators, and guidance counselors.
* Integration with existing school information systems, if available.


**Biggest Risk:**
* Inaccurate AI predictions resulting in unsuitable course recommendations.
* Incomplete or insufficient student academic and assessment data.
* Low adoption rate among students due to lack of awareness or trust in AI recommendations.
* Resistance from guidance counselors, faculty members, and administrators to adopting an AI-based system.
* Data privacy and security concerns involving sensitive student information.
* Students relying entirely on AI recommendations instead of considering their personal interests and career goals.
